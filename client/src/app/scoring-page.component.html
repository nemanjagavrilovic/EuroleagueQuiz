SCORING GUIDE

How points work

Every game contributes up to 100 ranked points. The four Grid levels share that game's 100-point total.

DAILY RANKED MAXIMUM600points across six games

Guest

Play a daily challenge for each game, with a separate grid at every level. Your daily scores are shown out of 100 and saved for this browser.

Guest results are excluded from the leaderboard, including rounds started before signing in.

Practice

Play fresh random challenges as often as you like. Practice results help you learn the games and are excluded from the leaderboard.

Ranked

Sign in to earn leaderboard points. Play one daily challenge per game and one grid at each of the four levels. Your results are saved to your account.

Seven correct squares on Hard earn 21 points. Completing all nine earns 30 points, including the bonus.

  • All four ranked levels add together for up to 100 Grid points each day.
  • You have unlimited guesses. Incorrect guesses do not deduct points.
  • Reveal keeps your earned points. Revealed squares earn zero, and the completion bonus requires nine correct guesses.

Guest grids award 10 daily points per correct square, plus 10 for all nine, for a maximum of 100 per grid. These guest scores are excluded from the leaderboard.

Starting Five

100 points maximum

Each correct player

Each player can be counted once per round. Incorrect guesses earn zero and the clock keeps running. When time runs out or you reveal a round, you keep the points from your correct guesses.

Your daily total

600 points maximum

The four Grid levels contribute up to 100 points together. Each of the other five games contributes up to 100, giving a maximum of 600 ranked points per day.

  • Daily challenges reset at 00:00 UTC. Refreshing a game restores your saved progress.
  • View standings for today, this week, this month, or all time. Weekly standings begin on Monday.
  • Equal points are ranked by completed challenges, then perfect results, then completion time. Matching results share a rank.
  • Results saved under earlier scoring rules keep their original leaderboard points.
